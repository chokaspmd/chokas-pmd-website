Our Story

From One Vision
to 40+ Projects.

In 2014, Angelo Chokas saw a gap in the Dallas Fort Worth construction market — existing firms lacked close attention to detail on their projects. He founded Chokas PMD Group to fix that.

What started as a commitment to doing things right has grown into a full-service development and property management firm with 15+ years of market expertise, 2M+ square feet developed, and a 98% client retention rate.

Today, our team manages ground-up light industrial development, property management, and investment opportunities — all with the same attention to detail and customer-first approach that started it all.
